Why These Guides Exist

Searching for help online during a crisis can be overwhelming. Many articles focus only on addiction itself, while others offer advice that feels judgmental or impossible to apply.

SoftLanding was created to offer calm, practical guidance for the real conversations and difficult moments that families, friends, and people in recovery experience every day.

Every guide is written to be compassionate, easy to understand, and focused on helping people move toward healthier choices— without shame.

SoftLanding provides free tools and educational resources for addiction, recovery, and supporting loved ones.
