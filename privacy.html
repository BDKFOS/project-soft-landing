Privacy

Privacy Policy.

Effective date: July 1, 2026

Project Soft Landing is designed to be simple and private to use. You do not need an account to use the site.

Information you provide

Some pages ask you to type details about your situation so the site can generate a response. This may include sensitive information about recovery, addiction, relationships, relapse, cravings, or family situations.

Do not enter information you are not comfortable submitting.

AI-generated responses

Some tools send your input to an AI service so a response can be generated. That means the text you enter may be processed by third-party AI infrastructure.

Accounts and databases

Project Soft Landing currently does not use user accounts, login profiles, or a user-facing database.

Analytics

The site may use basic hosting, performance, or analytics tools to understand site traffic and improve the experience. These tools may collect standard technical information such as browser type, device type, pages visited, referring pages, and approximate location.

Emergency information

Project Soft Landing is not an emergency service and does not monitor user input for emergencies. If you or someone else may be in immediate danger, contact emergency services or a crisis line now.

Children

This site is intended for general audiences and is not directed to children. If you are a minor and need help, consider contacting a trusted adult, emergency services, or a crisis line.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the site grows, especially if contact forms, analytics, accounts, or additional AI features are added.

Contact

A dedicated contact method may be added later. Until then, please do not use this site as a way to request emergency support.
