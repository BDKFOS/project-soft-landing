About

Small support for difficult moments.

Project Soft Landing is a collection of simple, private tools for people navigating recovery, sobriety, cravings, shame, hard conversations, and the people who love someone through addiction.

The site is built around one idea: people often do not need a whole life plan in a difficult moment. They need one softer place to land, one calmer sentence, one next step, or one minute of space before acting.

Project Soft Landing is not therapy, medical advice, legal advice, or emergency support. It is a practical support tool for reflection, communication, and small next steps.

What this site is for

  • Pausing during cravings, urges, shame, anxiety, or overwhelm.
  • Finding words for hard recovery conversations.
  • Choosing one small next step when things feel unclear.
  • Supporting someone you care about without carrying everything alone.

What this site is not

  • It is not a substitute for professional treatment.
  • It is not crisis intervention.
  • It is not a sponsor, therapist, doctor, lawyer, or emergency service.
  • It does not diagnose, treat, or guarantee outcomes.

Why it exists

Recovery is not only shaped by dramatic turning points. It is often shaped quietly: late at night, after a hard conversation, during boredom, loneliness, shame, exhaustion, or fear.

Project Soft Landing exists for those moments.
